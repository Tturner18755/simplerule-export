# SimpleRule — handoff brief

Exported 2026-10-11 from the live preview. There is no public share or publish URL. Stripe is not connected. The preview database is in-memory Postgres, not a bank.

## What it does

You enter monthly take-home, pick a percentage rule, and log spending by hand or by pasting amounts to the coach. No bank logins. Receipt photos are read once and not stored.

Three lanes:

- Need: rent or mortgage, groceries, utilities, gas, internet, cell, insurance, transit, minimum debt payments
- Want: dining, shopping, travel, hobbies, entertainment, extra subscriptions
- Save: 3-month cushion first (3 times this month's Needs cap), then extra debt payoff or investing. Minimum payments stay in Need.

Presets: 50/30/20 (default), 60/20/20, 70/20/10, or custom. Percents must sum to 100. Lane cap is take-home times that percent. Save gets the leftover cents so the three caps equal take-home.

Coach default name is Sage. Alternatives: Rue, Lane, Cal. It totals pasted spends and files them. It is not a financial advisor.
